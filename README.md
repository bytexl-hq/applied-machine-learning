# Applied Machine Learning

| S.No | Mini Project | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Pricing Health Insurance Premiums](mini-project-01) | Predict yearly medical insurance charges from a customer's application details with linear regression. Implement gradient descent from scratch, evaluate the model with regression metrics, diagnose its assumptions through residual analysis, and improve it with interaction features. |
| 2 | [Home Loan Approval Model](mini-project-02) | Predict home loan approvals for an Indian housing finance company with logistic regression. Interpret odds and odds ratios, implement the model from scratch, evaluate it with a confusion matrix and classification metrics, and choose a decision threshold for auto-approval. |
| 3 | [Bike Demand Under Dataset Shift](mini-project-03) | Forecast hourly bike-sharing demand with a model that must hold up on a later year's data. Control overfitting with L1 and L2 regularisation, tune hyperparameters with cross-validation, grid search and random search, and diagnose and correct dataset shift. |
| 4 | [Flight Fare Feature Engineering](mini-project-04) | Estimate domestic flight fares in India from raw, messy booking records. Clean fields and impute missing values, extract date, time and text features, compare categorical encodings including leakage-safe target encoding, and prune the feature set with filter, wrapper and embedded methods. |
| 5 | [Stroke Risk With Rare Positives](mini-project-05) | Identify patients at risk of stroke when only one in twenty records is positive. Rebalance training data with undersampling, oversampling and SMOTE, evaluate with precision-recall curves and balanced accuracy, and calibrate predicted probabilities. |
| 6 | [SMS Spam Filter](mini-project-06) | Filter spam text messages with probabilistic classifiers. Apply Bayes' theorem to word counts, compare Bernoulli, Multinomial and Gaussian naive Bayes, and analyse the words and errors behind the filter's decisions. |
| 7 | [Phone and Home Prices With KNN](mini-project-07) | Classify phones into price bands and estimate house values from similar known examples. Implement K-nearest neighbours from scratch, examine the effect of scaling and feature choice on distance, tune the metric and K, and apply KNN to regression. |
| 8 | [Tumours and Concrete With SVMs](mini-project-08) | Detect malignant tumours from cell measurements and predict concrete strength from mix ingredients with support vector machines. Examine maximum-margin geometry and the role of C, apply the kernel trick, and tune support vector regression. |
| 9 | [Readable Credit Risk Trees](mini-project-09) | Assess loan default risk with decision trees that stay interpretable, and predict car fuel efficiency with regression trees. Compute impurity and information gain by hand, implement CART splitting, and control overfitting with pre-pruning and cost-complexity pruning. |
| 10 | [Pricing Diamonds With Random Forests](mini-project-10) | Price certified diamonds from their grades and dimensions with tree ensembles. Compare single trees with voting ensembles, implement bagging from bootstrap samples, study how random forests decorrelate trees, and tune a forest with out-of-bag error. |
| 11 | [Boosting a Bank Campaign Model](mini-project-11) | Rank bank customers by their likelihood of subscribing to a term deposit. Contrast bagging with AdaBoost, implement gradient boosting from scratch, compare XGBoost, LightGBM and CatBoost, and balance learning rate against the number of trees. |
| 12 | [Retail Customer Segmentation](mini-project-12) | Group retail customers into actionable segments from purchase history and household details. Implement K-Means from scratch, choose the number of clusters, and compare density-based, hierarchical and Gaussian mixture clustering. |
| 13 | [Digit Compression and News Topics](mini-project-13) | Compress handwritten digit images and discover topics in news articles. Implement PCA from scratch, choose the number of components, compare PCA with t-SNE and UMAP embeddings, and model topics with Latent Dirichlet Allocation. |
| 14 | [Auditing an Income Model](mini-project-14) | Explain and audit a model that predicts whether a person earns over $50K. Compare permutation importance with SHAP, explain individual predictions with SHAP and LIME, communicate behaviour with partial dependence plots, and measure and mitigate gender bias. |
